delegation: one message, several workers

delegate takes one to four self-contained task texts and runs each in its own thread with its
own conversation, the same tools, and the same workspace. The reports come back into the
conversation that asked.

A worker cannot delegate. schemas(depth) simply does not offer the tool at depth 1, so the
tree is one level deep by construction rather than by a counter that a path might miss.

Workers do not stream into the card. The card shows that a worker started and what it was
asked; the reports arrive as part of the answer.
